# The landing page — `/`

Mode: Persuade. Public, signed in or not (plan §21; D397 – D400).

## Job
A clinic owner — often a doctor — deciding whether to try ClinicDesk, on a phone or a desk. One action: **Start free trial**; **Sign in** beside it; someone signed in is offered **Open ClinicDesk** instead.

## Proof and content
Real screenshots of a demo clinic made and deleted by `scripts/landing-shots.mts`, in light and dark, each theme's shown to that theme's visitors (D401), and marked as a made-up clinic. The live trial days and the two prices from platform settings, read at request time; the renewal contact (Call, WhatsApp, Email) once set. No testimonials, usage numbers, ratings or logos — the product has none.

## States
A part of the contact unset is hidden; a trial of 0 days drops the "days free"; settings that cannot be read leave the prices line out (never a default shown as live); signed in → Open ClinicDesk (Home, or the console for the DEV).
